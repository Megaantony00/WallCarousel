WallCarousel keeps the home and lock screens of your tablet fresh by rotating the photos you choose.

Import photos from the system gallery. WallCarousel copies them into its private storage, so the originals in your gallery are never modified or deleted.

Features:
- separate portrait and landscape versions of the same photo;
- non-destructive adjustments: focal point, zoom, brightness, contrast, saturation and blur;
- ordered carousels for the home screen and the lock screen;
- independent scheduling per screen: an interval of 15 minutes or more, specific times of day, an advance on every unlock, or any combination of them;
- Quick Settings tiles to advance the wallpaper manually;
- adaptive layout for tablets, DeX, split-screen and pop-up view;
- English and Italian interface.

Privacy:
- no internet permission, no analytics, no ads, no tracking;
- everything is processed and stored on the device;
- you can export a backup or delete all data at any time from the settings.
